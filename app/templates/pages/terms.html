Acceptance

Platform Compliance

The tool interacts with Udemy's enrollment endpoints using your own session tokens. Automated activity on Udemy may face rate limits, CAPTCHAs, or other restrictions imposed by the platform. The maintainers cannot guarantee that any feature remains available.

Credential Safety

  • Session cookies are encrypted with Fernet before storage.
  • Your Udemy password is never stored — not even as a hash.
  • Credentials are sent only to Udemy's enrollment endpoints and are never sold or shared with third parties.
  • The hosted demo uses Cookie Login only — email-and-password login is available only when self-hosting.

Rate Limiting & Respectful Use

Enrollment requests are deliberately paced to be respectful of Udemy's platform: 6–15 seconds between requests in server mode and 3–8 seconds in local mode. Circuit breakers trip on repeated 403 responses to protect your account.

Do not reduce these delays. The pacing is a technical choice, not a guarantee against account limits, and users remain responsible for their own usage.

Known Limitations

  • Coupons expire rapidly and may already be gone when a listing is published.
  • Not all 100%-off coupons successfully enroll — some are invalid, regional, or already claimed.
  • Udemy may change its flow at any time, breaking parts of the automation.
  • Impact metrics use list prices — actual savings depend on the prices Udemy shows at enrollment time.
  • There is no guarantee for any course or coupon.

Data Stored vs. Never Stored

Stored

  • Encrypted session cookies (Fernet), used only to communicate with Udemy.
  • Your preferences and aggregate enrollment history for the dashboard.
  • Session expiration metadata so old sessions can be cleaned up. Hosted demo sessions expire after about 24 hours.

Never Stored

  • Your password — not even a hash. It is sent directly to Udemy and discarded.
  • Payment information — Udemy Enroller never handles or stores any payment details.
  • Browsing history or any tracking of activity outside the app itself.

Browser Automation

Coupon aggregator sources are accessed with CloudScraper (HTTP) or Playwright (browser fallback) using respectful rate limits.

Udemy Enroller does not bypass CAPTCHAs, rotate proxies, or mass-scrape. A circuit breaker self-blocks the source after consecutive errors to avoid hammering sites.
