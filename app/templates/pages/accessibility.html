Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Commitment

Udemy Enroller aims to make public pages usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technologies. We target Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA for public marketing and documentation surfaces. This is a continuous improvement goal, not a formal third-party certification claim.

Measures we take

  • Semantic HTML landmarks, heading hierarchy, and skip-to-content link.
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation and primary controls on marketing pages.
  • Text alternatives for primary brand imagery where provided.
  • Consent and legal pages available without requiring analytics cookies.

Known limitations

  • Authenticated dashboards: enrollment progress tables and dense tooling may not yet meet every AA criterion.
  • Third-party services: analytics (after consent) and external links to Udemy or GitHub are outside full first-party control.

Feedback and assistance

If you encounter an accessibility barrier—or need content in an alternative format—please contact us via the contact page or the founder hub at madhudadi.in. Include the page URL, assistive technology (if any), and a short description.
